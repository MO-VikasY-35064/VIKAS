Good — 2-Step Verification is already on, which is exactly what's needed to generate an App Password.

Since Google removed the direct "App passwords" link from this page for some accounts, here's how to reach it:

Go directly to https://myaccount.google.com/apppasswords
It may ask you to sign in again (security check).
Under App name, type something like TailoringWebApp and click Create.
Google will show a 16-character password (e.g. abcd efgh ijkl mnop) — copy it exactly (you can keep or remove the spaces, both work).
This is a one-time display — if you navigate away without copying it, you'll need to generate a new one.

If that direct link doesn't work for you (sometimes Google gates it based on account type — e.g. Workspace accounts, or very new accounts), try:

Security & sign-in (left sidebar, which you're already on) → scroll down past "Password" and "2-Step Verification" — there's usually a dedicated "App passwords" row further down that same page, sometimes not visible in your screenshot's crop
Or search "App passwords" in the search bar at the top of the Google Account page

Once you have the 16-character password, put it in .env:

SMTP_USERNAME=yourgmailaddress@gmail.com
SMTP_PASSWORD=abcdefghijklmnop
