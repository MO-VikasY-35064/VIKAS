How It Works

1. Sign Up & Measure

Create an account with your mobile number and enter your measurements once. We keep a version history so future orders always use the right numbers.

2. Choose a Design

Browse our catalog, pick a style, and add any customisation notes.

3. Pay Your Way

Pay online, at the shop, or after delivery - whatever works best for you.

4. Cutting & Stitching

We keep you posted as your order moves through cutting and stitching.

5. Trial

Once your trial piece is ready, we email you a notification with a photo.

6. Delivery

After any alterations, your finished blouse is ready for delivery on your chosen date.
